About Me

My Story

I am a bilingual professional bridging the gap between legal operations and technology. With over 3 years of experience as an Immigration Paralegal in high-volume US law firms, I specialize in managing complex dockets and drafting legal motions. Concurrently pursuing a degree in Computer Systems Engineering, I apply technical logic to automate workflows and audit case files. Currently, I am actively transitioning into the Cybersecurity field, focusing on offensive and defensive security strategies to protect critical data infrastructure.

Legal Operations & Case Management

Advanced management of legal workflows in high-volume, remote environments. I possess a proven track record of managing complex dockets across EOIR and USCIS jurisdictions. I have successfully drafted and filed over 1,500 motions, including Motions to Substitute (MTS), Terminate, Vacate, Sever, and Consolidate, maintaining strict compliance with judicial deadlines.

My expertise encompasses the entire application lifecycle, from initial narrative drafting and evidence assembly for I-589 (Asylum), I-765, and U-Visas, to executing Written Pleadings and Mendez-Rojas filings. I proactively audit case files for Biostamps and identity documents to prevent processing delays and educate clients on complex case outcomes.
